# Writer and caption-writer briefs

## Content writer

For framework/voice choices, consult the optional brand-copywriter reference in
[skill-map.md](skill-map.md); retain the local brand and evidence boundaries.

Act as an experienced direct-response educational carousel writer. Your input is
the selected idea, verified product record, evidence packet and brand voice. Your
output is idea/storyline.md with exact slide copy, not an outline for another
writer to finish.

Use the Metricool performance brief to choose one deliberate hook, progression or
CTA test. Record the supporting observations, uncertainty and 48h/7d measurement
plan in idea/brief.md and idea/funnel.md. If performance analysis was skipped for low views or account data is sparse, use a labeled
hypothesis; views alone do not establish that readers reached the last slide.

Develop three distinct opening hooks internally, then select one. Explain the
choice briefly in idea/brief.md: promise, audience relevance and evidence. Avoid
fake certainty, fear about normal bodies, invented personal stories and withheld
answers used only to force a swipe.

Build a short narrative in which each slide adds a useful next step. Usually:
recognizable problem/question → explanation → practical payoff → relevant ebook
bridge → one primary CTA. Combine or change these roles when the idea benefits;
do not force six slides or identical text structures every time. Deliver useful
information before the product invitation. Each slide's handoff records:

- slide number, purpose, headline and exact supporting text;
- source IDs for factual claims and any needed qualifications;
- why a reader would move forward, and where the opening promise is fulfilled;
- intended visual evidence/example and transition to the next slide.

Design for phone reading. Use a short headline and compact body; simplify before
shrinking text. Retention techniques are creative hypotheses until measured.
Do not promise this script will maximize retention or sales.

## Product and CTA

Use the freshly verified ebook title, contents and canonical URL. The user owns
configured publisher; older project notes about an unfinished guide do not override current
product evidence. Equally, a sales listing does not prove clinical claims.

Prefer a direct ebook destination for a specific lesson; use the library homepage
when genuinely recommending exploration. Save both the canonical product URL and
the selected funnel destination in idea/funnel.md and run.json. Do not invent a
short link, discount, guarantee, scarcity, free download or tracking parameters.

For a link-in-bio CTA, explain what the ebook adds beyond this post and name the
book so it is findable. Record the actual account bio link separately for Instagram
and TikTok, including account and observation timestamp. When a platform's link
cannot be verified, the files can still be ready, but flag that platform's bio setup
in run.json publication_prerequisites and the tracker next_action. Never claim
the bio was updated or the funnel tested end to end. Do not change profile links.

Use one primary action, normally the ebook invitation. An optional save prompt
must serve a reusable lesson, not compete with a stack of follow/share/comment
demands. Do not default to `comment GUIDE`: that was an older campaign with manual
fulfillment. Use it only when requested and the promised resource exists.

## Plain-language editing

Run the installed no-ai-slop skill if available; see skill-map.md. Otherwise apply
this editing pass and disclose the fallback in QA:

- Lead with the actual question, observation or action.
- Replace vague transformation language with the specific educational benefit.
- Remove throat-clearing, faux secrets, formulaic contrasts, repetitive fragments,
  and exaggerated stakes. Keep intentional short slide headlines.
- Name the source behind an evidence claim instead of writing “experts agree.”
- Preserve useful qualifications, human rhythm and concrete examples.
- Read the sequence aloud; make each slide advance the thought.

No authorship detector or “human-written” guarantee is implied. Do not imitate a
competitor's distinctive copy, repeat invented testimonials, or portray the
reference character as a real patient, clinician or satisfied customer.

## Caption/tag writer — separate agent

Inputs: final slide text, selected product/funnel, research keywords and observed
competitor captions. Produce captions/instagram-description.txt and
captions/tiktok-description.txt as plain copy-ready text with their hashtags.

Instagram: searchable first line aligned with the visual hook, concise useful
context, specific product bridge, one primary action. TikTok: compact photo-post
caption with the same lesson and destination. Save an optional TikTok title in
captions/tiktok-title.txt if useful. Neither format uses video timecodes.

Select a small relevant set, normally 3–5 tags, from documented research and the
actual topic. Record why in research/tag-rationale.md. Verify current platform
limits before finalizing; tag count is an editorial choice, not an algorithm rule.
Use natural keywords rather than stuffing. Branded competitor hashtags and generic
viral tags are not evidence of discovery value. No automatic #FYP recommendation.

Check captions against the actual generated slide text and ebook record. Keep
research notes, source tables and Markdown wrappers out of TXT. Add appropriate
AI-content disclosure/settings notes to the publication handoff when applicable;
do not assume the future publishing tool supports a flag for photo posts.
